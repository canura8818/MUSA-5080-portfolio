Key concepts

Joining

  • join function: joins two tables by a column for rows that match
  • left/right_join: keep all observations in x/y
  • inner_join: only keep observations that are in both
  • full_join: keep observations in both
  • anti_join: keep observations that don’t match between two tables

Geometry

  • shp: shape file
  • gdb: geodatabase
  • sf: simple features

Coordinate reference systems (CRS)

The Earth is a geoid, which is an imperfect circle, that can be approximated with an ellipsoid (projection). Different flattening ratios for each CRS.

  • NAD27: Clarke’s ellipsoid marked at Meades Ranch, KS (degrees)
  • NAD83: GRS-80 reference system, Earth-centered (degrees)
  • WGS84: Earth-centered (GPS standard)
  • Albers equal area (meters)

Ways to project

  1. cylindrical: based on latitude with no distortion
  2. transverse cylindrical: based on longitude with straight line of tangency
  3. conic: curved line of tangency
  4. planar: circular line of tangency
  5. conformal conic: good for high latitude countries

what to preserve and what projection to use

  • Shapes: conformal conic (especially good for high latitude countries)
  • Areas: equal-area
  • Distance: equal-distance
  • Direction: :(

Geographic - units:degrees

Projected - good for local mapping - units: feet` or meters

R

  • st_set_crs(): relabel
  • st_transform(): change CRS
  • st_crs(): get CRS
  • st_buffer(): inner buffer of geometry
  • st_intersects(): do two geometry objects intersect
  • st_touches(): one point in common but not intersecting
  • st_within(): does every of A lie in B
  • st_filter(): subset by spatial feature
  • st_intersection(): returns intersection of two geometries
  • st_union(): merge two geometries with no overlap
  • st_join(): combine two objects based on geometric relationship
  • st_distance(): return minimum distance between two objects
